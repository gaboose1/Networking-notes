# Networking Basics

## What Is a Computer Network?

A computer network is a group of connected devices that communicate and share data and resources with each other.

Examples of network devices include computers, smartphones, servers, printers, switches, routers, and access points.

## Main Types of Networks

### LAN

LAN stands for Local Area Network. It connects devices within a limited area such as a home, school, office, or building.

### WAN

WAN stands for Wide Area Network. It connects networks across large geographical areas.

The Internet is the largest example of a WAN.

### WLAN

WLAN stands for Wireless Local Area Network. It is a LAN that uses wireless communication, usually through Wi-Fi.

## Network Communication

Devices on a network use protocols and addressing systems to communicate.

Common concepts include:

- IP addresses
- MAC addresses
- Network protocols
- Ports
- DNS
- DHCP

## Why Networking Matters

Networking allows devices to communicate, share resources, access services, and connect to the Internet.
